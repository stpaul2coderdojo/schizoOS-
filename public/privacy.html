2. Data Collection & Stateless Processing

  • Ephemeral Alexa Voice Intents: Spoken utterances sent to the Vayu Vaidya Wallmiki skill are converted by Amazon Alexa into structured intents (e.g., 4-4-6-2 Pranayama breathing, 5-4-3-2-1 sensory grounding, autopilot loop check-in) and processed statelessly in memory by our AWS Lambda endpoint.

  • Login with Amazon (LWA) Consent: OAuth credentials and tokens issued via Login with Amazon are used strictly to authorize your requested Amazon Alexa integration and are never shared with third parties.

3. Data Sharing & Security
