## 1. Overview & Purpose of This Consent Privacy Notice

## 2. Information We Collect & Process

We adhere strictly to a **Privacy-by-Design and Zero-Persistent-PHI (Protected Health Information)** architecture:

1. **Amazon Alexa Voice & Intent Data:**
   - When you interact with the Vayu Vaidya Wallmiki skill on Amazon Alexa, Amazon converts your spoken utterances into text intents (such as requesting a 4-4-6-2 Pranayama breathing exercise, sensory grounding, autopilot check-in, or therapeutic reflection) and forwards the structured request to our AWS Lambda endpoint.
   - We process these requests **ephemerally in memory** solely to generate the immediate therapeutic voice response.
   - We **do not** store, record, sell, or build persistent personal profiles from your voice interactions.

2. **Login with Amazon (LWA) & OAuth Consent Data:**
   - Where Login with Amazon (LWA) is used for developer automation (such as AWS CodePipeline skill deployment) or optional account linking, authentication tokens are used strictly for authorizing the requested Amazon Alexa / ASK services and are never shared with third parties.

3. **Web Application Local State (`schizoOS` Web Sanctuary):**
   - Interactive art therapy canvas drawings, somatic body scan selections, and breathing session metrics remain local to your browser session unless you explicitly export them to your own device.

## 3. How We Use Information

Information processed during an active session is used exclusively to:
- Deliver real-time guided **Vayu Vaidya Pranayama (4-4-6-2)** vagal breathing exercises.
- Provide **5-4-3-2-1 sensory grounding** and **MiCBT interoceptive body scan** guidance.
- Facilitate **schizoOS cognitive architecture** reflection (*Manas, Chitta, Buddhi, Bodhichitta* vs. subconscious *Autopilot* loops).
- Generate non-judgmental, supportive CBT conversational responses via our secure backend.

## 6. Data Retention & Your Revocation Rights

- **Zero Persistent Retention:** Because voice and chat prompts are processed statelessly in memory, no personal conversation history is retained on our servers after your session ends.
- **Revoking Consent:** You may revoke consent at any time by disabling the **Vayu Vaidya Wallmiki** skill in your Amazon Alexa App or revoking access in your Amazon Account under **Login with Amazon > Manage Apps**.
